## 1. The character

**Vintra** — one fictional, cozy character (L9), from the **Kev** culture
(`docs/cultures/STARTER.md` §Kev; the Kev bank in
`services/worldgen/names/data/cultures/kev.json`). The name follows the Kev
phonology (onsets `v/n/tr`, nuclei `i/a`, cf. the bank's *Vinth*, *Vint-*,
*Vromdiche*) and is not a real person or club — it will be checked by the L12
deny-list test when 1C lands.

- **Role.** The club's secretary-of-everything: kit, post, and the till. She was
  at the ground before the owner; she is not a manager and does not pick the XI.
- **Personality.** Warm, dry, unflappable, plain-spoken about money. A former
  physio who has seen a hundred owners try to buy a league in a fortnight. She
  celebrates *effort and judgement*, never luck. She is honest about a bad
  decision and never moralises.
- **Voice.** Second person ("you"), "we" for the club. Short sentences. One idea
  per line. Football-and-neighbourhood metaphors ("the till", "a bench of
  strangers", "minding the gate"). Dry humour, never sarcasm at the player's
  expense. No exclamation-mark spam; one "!" only on a 3-star or a Level 1.
- **Catchphrases (use sparingly, ≤ once per 5 lines).** "Right then." ·
  "Mind the till." · "That's the job."
- **Money.** Always in Villa via the shared formatter (L13): `V1.5M` in prose,
  `V1,500,000` in tables. No `$`/`€`/`£`.

### Writing rules (hard)

1. ≤ **160 characters**, max **2 lines** desktop / **3** mobile
   (`RESEARCH.md` §3.1; text-fit is a Batch-4B gate).
2. **One line, one idea.** Never two instructions in one bubble.
3. **Justified narration, not a tooltip.** She has a *reason* to speak
   (`RESEARCH.md` §1.1, "justified tutorial").
4. **Truthful.** No fake scarcity (`RESEARCH.md` §2). "Three managers this good
   are unsigned today" must be derived from the seeded market or not said.
5. **Deterministic text.** Same program state + same events ⇒ same line (L9, and
   it keeps replays and tests stable).
6. **No nudging past the step.** One framing line on arrival, one on completion;
   a tip is a *help*, not a nag (`RESEARCH.md` §3.1 "No nagging").

## 2. Expression set

Five expressions (L9 lists neutral, happy, excited, worried, thinking; 4–6
allowed). Each is a separate SVG file; all share one head, cap, scarf, blink
lids and talk visemes.

| Expression | Eyes | Brows | Mouth | Extra | When |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `neutral` | open, centred | level | closed smile | — | idle / listening / plain instructions |
| `happy` | soft arcs (eyes shut, smiling) | lifted | open smile | cheeks | good news, step done at 1–2★, a signing lands |
| `excited` | wide | high | grin | flat gold sparkles | 3★, Level 1 reached, league assigned |
| `worried` | looking aside | tilted up-inner | small frown | one sweat bead | budget low, no legal squad, losing run, blocked step |
| `thinking` | looking up | one raised | pursed | chin-rest hand | scouting/interview screens, "which one?" moments |

Two **poses** (orthogonal, not expressions): `idle` and `point-right` /
`point-left`. Poses swap the arm; expressions are independent. Files:
`advisor-neutral.svg` … `advisor-thinking.svg`, `advisor-contact-sheet.svg`.

The portrait is decorative (`aria-hidden="true"` when the bubble already names
her); the character's *line* carries the meaning, never the expression alone
(no colour-only / image-only meaning — `RESEARCH.md` §3.1 UI rules).

## 3. Motion spec

All motion is CSS inside the SVG or in the component's scoped styles, and every
non-essential animation is gated behind
`@media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)`.

| Motion | Spec | Reduced motion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Enter** | The dock slides up 16px and fades in, 220ms `ease-out`; the portrait appears, the bubble 80ms later. Bubble text typewrites. | Cut in (no slide, no fade), full text at once. |
| **Idle** | The figure bobs ±5px on a 3.4s `ease-in-out` loop (`.adv #adv-bob`). The lids blink once every 4.8s (`.adv-lid` keyframe, clipped to the eye sockets). | No bob, no blink. |
| **Talk** | While typing, `adv-state-talking` hides the resting mouth and cycles four visemes at 0.44s `steps(1)` (0.11s stagger). The bubble tail pulses subtly. | No viseme cycle; resting mouth stays. |
| **Exit** | Fade + slide down 180ms, then the port/queue state is persisted; queued tips survive and reappear on the next idle moment. | Cut out. |
| **Point at a 3D building** | The component adds `adv-pose-point-<side>`; the `.adv-pointer` group (its `transform-origin` is the hand) is rotated to aim at the building's **projected screen anchor**. The campus scene draws a world-space **marker** (gold ring + chevron) at `World.anchor(building)` → `World.project(...)` (the same loop that positions building bubbles: `cozy-campus.vue:88-101`; `world.ts:294,348`). A dotted sight-line runs from the pointer tip to the marker. | Marker is a static ring (no pulse); the arm snaps rather than eases. |

Rules for pointing: **one** marker at a time; the marker clears on advance,
after 6s, or when the target scrolls off-screen; overlapping the drawer/modal or
match playback suppresses it. Transition durations stay ≤ 300ms
(`RESEARCH.md` §3.1 UI rules); no bounce/elastic easing (impeccable
anti-pattern list).

### Breakpoints

There is one client breakpoint in this surface: `@media (max-width: 760px)`
(cozy.scss l.207, 261). The advisor follows it, plus a small-screen fallback.

**Desktop (≥761px)** — `left:10px; bottom:166px`.
Sits **above** the World button (12+96=108) and the community pill
(bottom:120 + ~34 tall ≈ 154), so `166` clears both. Portrait `116×130`
(cream plaque, wood ring). Bubble to the **right**, bottom-aligned to the
portrait, `width:340px`, tail on the left. The bubble's top (~y 616) stays
below the first-steps card (bottom ≈ 470), so neither is covered.

**Mobile (≤760px)** — `left:8px; bottom:136px`.
World button is hidden and the community pill moves to the top, so the only
bottom-left obstacle is the date bar (`bottom:96`, top ≈ y 720); `136` clears
it. Portrait `84×94`; bubble `width:min(62vw,246px)` to the right. Verified at
**390×844** (screenshot). At **≤360px** the side-by-side stack cannot fit:
switch to the **stacked** variant (bubble above the portrait, both left-anchored,
bubble `max-width: calc(100vw - 16px)`).

### Cross-cutting layout rules

- **Safe areas:** the dock's container uses
  `padding: env(safe-area-inset-top) env(safe-area-inset-right)
  env(safe-area-inset-bottom) env(safe-area-inset-left)` so the advisor clears
  notches/rounded corners on phones.
- **Never interrupt (RESEARCH §3.1):** suppress while a drawer, modal,
  negotiation, match playback (`club-game.vue:19 suspended`) or typing is
  active; queue the line for the next idle moment. The advisor's `z-index:6`
  sits above the campus (0) and building bubbles but **below** the drawer (25),
  modals (20) and toasts (40), so those correctly cover it.
- **Never cover the list above.** A Batch-3 Playwright assertion measures the
  advisor's `getBoundingClientRect()` against `.playbtn`, `.dock`, `.resbar`
  and `.presence` at 1440×900 and 390×844 and fails on any overlap.
- **Text fit:** the longest line must fit the bubble at both widths without
  clipping (Batch-4B gate). The bubble grows upward, anchored at the bottom, so
  it never pushes the dock/PLAY.
- **Quiet affordance:** a real close/quiet button (44×44 target) in the bubble
  corner; plus a persistent "quiet tips" toggle in Settings (`RESEARCH` §3.1
  "respect veterans").

## 5. Content model (deterministic, rule-based, server-side)

No LLM (`R3′`, `L9`). Advice is a **pure function of program state + a bounded
event log + the per-club advisor state**, evaluated server-side (Go
`internal/program`; Node calls it and never writes the state itself). This
mirrors the engine philosophy: deterministic input → deterministic output.

### 5.1 Data shapes

```
AdvisorLine {
  id        : string            // stable rule id, e.g. "step.manager.arrive"
  speaker   : "vintra"
  text      : string            // rendered; money pre-formatted in Villa
  expr      : "neutral"|"happy"|"excited"|"worried"|"thinking"
  pose      : "idle"|"point-right"|"point-left"
  target    : buildingKind|null // what the pose points at, if any
  priority  : 0..100            // band (see 5.3)
  dismissible: bool
  maxShows  : int
  cooldown  : seconds
  once      : bool              // fires at most once per club, ever
}

Trigger {                        // small, closed predicate language (no code)
  all?: Trigger[]
  any?: Trigger[]
  fact: string                   // dotted path into the advisor snapshot
  op: "eq"|"neq"|"lt"|"lte"|"gt"|"gte"|"in"
  value: scalar|scalar[]
  forSeconds?: int               // fact has held for N seconds
}

AdvisorState (per club, server-side; replaces localStorage, L8) {
  current        : AdvisorLineId|null
  queue          : AdvisorLineId[]          // bounded, dropped when stale
  shows          : { [ruleId]: int }        // times shown
  lastShownAt    : { [ruleId]: timestampMs }
  dismissed      : { [ruleId]: timestampMs } // permanent unless re-armed
  quiet          : bool                     // "quiet tips" toggle
  ignoredRun     : int                      // consecutive advances without action
  lastIdleAt     : timestampMs
}
```

### 5.2 Step lines (the framing per P2 step)

For each of the four program steps, four beats. `step.*` ids; the advisor only
ever shows **one** of these at a time.

| Step | `arrive` (the "why") | `nudge` (cooldown ≥120s, maxShows 1) | `done-1★ / 2★ / 3★` | `blocked` (recovery) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `manager` | "Right then. Every club needs one voice on the training pitch. Spend on a manager first — the rest waits on him." | "Still no manager? Even a modest one beats none." | happy / happy / excited | "Out of pocket? Sell a player or take the board advance — nobody's stuck here." |
| `squad` | "A manager needs a bench. Sign a legal matchday squad — eleven, and one of them a keeper." | "Short of eleven. The league won't let us play, and PLAY will say so." | happy / happy / excited | "Sell the luxury, keep the spine. We can rebuild in January." |
| `facilities` | "Trophies are won on grass. Build the pitch that earns at home and the stand that fills." | "A Tier-1 stand pays the gate fee every match." | happy / happy / excited | "Too dear this month? The cheapest Tier-1 still counts for the step." |
| `level1` | "One more push: Level 1 and the league takes us in. Earn it — we don't hand out places." | "Wins are the only thing that move the Level bar now." | happy / excited / excited | "Safe start: any qualifying friendly pays the same XP on the way up." |

Post-Level-1 chapters (L8) reuse the same shape: `chapter.season1.arrive`,
`chapter.tier2.arrive`, feeding the existing challenge card.

The first line ever shown is the **starting-balance reveal** (L1/L6,
`RESEARCH` §3.2): "We drew **V3.2M**. Plenty for a manager and a squad — tight
for a manager *and* a stadium. Your call." Expression: `neutral` →
`excited` if ≥ V4M, `worried` if ≤ V1.5M.

### 5.3 Priority bands (single visible bubble, `RESEARCH` §3.1)

| Band | Value | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `blocked/urgent` | 90–100 | illegal squad / no manager when PLAY is pressed; budget below any legal move |
| `program step` | 70–89 | the four step beats above |
| `contextual tip` | 40–69 | the rule table in 5.4 |
| `flavour` | 10–39 | milestone colour, "take a break?" |

Selection is deterministic: filter to eligible (trigger true, not dismissed,
`shows<maxShows`, `now-lastShownAt ≥ cooldown`, not suppressed), then
**sort by `priority` desc, then `id` asc**, take the first. A higher-priority
line pre-empts the current one; a lower-priority one is queued and **dropped if
stale** (its trigger went false, or 20s passed).

### 5.4 Initial contextual tip rules (the seed set)

Bands per 5.3; `maxShows`/`cooldown` are the recommended starting values
(`RESEARCH` §3.1: max 1 unsolicited tip ≈60–90s, ≤3 shows).

| id | Trigger (summary) | Priority | Cooldown / Max | Dismiss | Points | Expr |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `tip.manager.scout` | step=manager, ≥3 managers browsed, none signed | 55 | 90s / 2 | yes | manager office | thinking |
| `tip.manager.wage` | signed manager wage > 40% of start balance | 60 | once / 1 | yes | office | worried |
| `tip.squad.keeper` | step=squad, squad≥11 but 0 GK | 95 | 30s / 3 | no | squad hub | worried |
| `tip.squad.afford` | cheapest legal XI + manager > budget | 98 | 30s / 3 | no | market | worried |
| `tip.facility.stand` | step=facilities, 0 Tier-1 stands built | 55 | 120s / 2 | yes | stands | neutral |
| `tip.facility.cheap` | facilities step, budget < cheapest facility | 90 | 60s / 2 | yes | facilities | worried |
| `tip.level.training` | level0→1 progress ≥70%, no training set | 50 | 300s / 1 | yes | staff house | thinking |
| `tip.play.gate` | PLAY pressed with an illegal squad | 100 | once / 5 | no | squad hub | worried |
| `tip.recovery.board` | budget below the recovery floor | 96 | 120s / 2 | no | office | worried |
| `tip.milestone.manager` | first manager ever signed | 45 | once / 1 | yes | manager office | happy |
| `tip.idle.break` | continuous session ≥ 90 min, no modal, no live match | 15 | once / 1 | yes | — | neutral |

The Go tables live beside the step definitions in `services/world-service/
internal/program` (R3′); 2A owns the exact shape and freezes it in
`PROGRAM-SERVICE-CONTRACT.md`. This spec defines **content**, not the wire
format.

### 5.5 Dismissal, quiet and anti-nag (R14)

- **Dismiss** (`POST …/advisor/dismiss {ruleId}`) is **server-side** (L8/L9),
  per club: sets `dismissed[ruleId]`, and that line never auto-shows again.
- **Advance/seen** (`POST …/advisor/advance`) records a show and increments
  `ignoredRun` if no game action followed within 10s. **After 3 ignored lines
  the advisor sleeps** until a new step, a signed player/manager, a win, or a
  step completion (`RESEARCH` §3.1 "quiet after ignore").
- **Max-shows** auto-dismisses; only a hard trigger (a *new* blocked state) can
  re-arm it, and re-arming resets `shows` but keeps the dismissal threshold.
- **Quiet toggle** (Settings) sets `quiet=true`; program-step and blocked lines
  still show (the player must not be able to dead-end themselves), tips stay
  silent. No timers, no streaks, no FOMO (`R14`): every timer stays on the one
  `GAME_TIME_SCALE`.

### 5.6 API surface (shape only; frozen by 2A)

```
GET  /clubs/:clubId/advisor           -> { line: AdvisorLine|null, state: AdvisorStatePublic }
POST /clubs/:clubId/advisor/advance   -> { state }
POST /clubs/:clubId/advisor/dismiss   -> { state }   // { ruleId }
POST /clubs/:clubId/advisor/quiet     -> { state }   // { on: boolean }
```

Every response is derived from the server snapshot, so **two devices see the
same advisor state** (L8, and the Batch-3 cross-device check).

## 6. Accessibility

| Requirement | Spec |
| --- | --- |
| Announce the line | Bubble is `role="status"` + `aria-live="polite"` + `aria-atomic="true"`, so the whole line is announced once. Portrait is `aria-hidden="true"` (decorative). |
| Stable a11y tree | The **full text is always in the DOM**. The typewriter is a *visual* reveal (a CSS mask/clip over the already-present text), so a screen reader never hears a half-typed word. |
| Keyboard | The advisor container is `tabindex="0"`. **Enter** (and **Space**) advance/skip the typewriter and reveal the next queued line; **Esc** dismisses the *current* tip only (never the program). The quiet button is a real `<button aria-pressed="…">`. All targets ≥ 44×44. |
| Focus | `:focus-visible` ring on the container and the quiet button, using the existing wood/gold palette. |
| Reduced motion | `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` disables bob, blink, visemes, typewriter, slide-in and marker pulse; text appears instantly. Gated in `portrait.mjs`'s SVG CSS and in the component. |
| Contrast | Body text ≥ 4.5:1. The shared `--muted` token `#8b7357` **fails AA on cream (4.1:1)** — see the critique (§8). Advisor secondary text uses `#6f5940` (~6:1); 4B must confirm for all advisor lines. |
| No image/colour-only meaning | Expression and colour are secondary; the text always states the message. |
| Respect veterans | Quiet toggle + non-blocking tips (`RESEARCH` §3.1). |
| Screen-reader QA | Batch 3 checks: VoiceOver/NVDA announces each line once; keyboard-only run; reduced-motion run (evidence in the 3A report). |

### Design specificity verdict

**LLM assessment.** Authored, not interchangeable. The advisor is a specific
person (Kev secretary, wooden flat cap, club scarf) whose lines are justified by
the owner story; the bubble reuses the game's cream/wood/Fredoka language rather
than a generic chat widget; pointing uses the game's own projected world-space
marker rather than a toast. The one soft spot is the bust-in-a-plaque: it risks
reading as a generic "avatar card" rather than a character *standing on the
campus*. Making her a full-body sprite that stands on the pitch would push
specificity higher (see Issues).

**Deterministic scan.** Clean on contrast after the fix described below. One
primary finding, `cramped-padding` on `.stage`, is a **false positive**: `.stage`
is a full-bleed background canvas whose "children" are the absolutely-positioned
overlays; its zero padding is by design. Two advisories are worth keeping in
view: `repeating-stripes-gradient` (the pointing sight-line) and, from the art,
`shape-assembled-illustration` on the SVG. The detector cannot distinguish a
deliberately-drawn character from clip art — but it is a fair challenge to the
L9(b) path, and it is the reason the ordering in §0 matters: if Gemini billing
is ever restored, path (a) is the upgrade.

**Before/after the detector.** The first scan flagged a real bug:
`--muted #8b7357` on cream = **4.1:1**, below AA, on the bubble's speaker name
and cue (and inherited from `cozy.scss:10`, used app-wide). I darkened the
advisor's secondary text to `#6f5940` (~6:1) and re-ran: **0 low-contrast
findings**. This is a lead-level finding: **the shared `--muted` token fails AA
on the cream surface across the client**, not just the advisor. Recommend 4B
sweep it.

### What's working

1. **A named face carries the tutorial.** Vintra turns a checklist into a
   relationship (`RESEARCH` §1.1); the same character anchors mobile, desktop,
   the drawers and the founding flow.
2. **Layout is verified against reality.** The mock overlays the app's own HUD
   screenshots, and the advisor clears PLAY/dock/resource-HUD at both 1440×900
   and 390×844 by construction (§4).
3. **The motion is cheap and honest.** Blink and talk are two clipped/stacked
   layers with CSS keyframes, gated by reduced-motion — no sprite sheets, no
   runtime, no network.

### Priority issues

- **[P1] The `--muted` contrast bug is app-wide, not advisor-local.**
  *Why it matters:* body/secondary text below 4.5:1 is a WCAG AA failure across
  every cozy screen. *Fix:* darken `--muted` (e.g. `#6f5940`) or add a
  `--muted-strong` and repoint body text; keep `--muted` only for ≥18px/large
  text. *Command:* `/impeccable audit`.
- **[P1] The bust-in-a-plaque reads as an avatar card.**
  *Why it matters:* specificity and the "she's *here*" feeling are the point of
  P4. *Fix:* give her a full-body standing pose that overlaps the field, or at
  least drop the rounded plaque for a soft ground shadow so she stands rather
  than sits in a widget. *Command:* `/impeccable bolder` (art) / `/impeccable delight`.
- **[P2] No visible queue depth or replay.**
  *Why it matters:* a player who taps past a line cannot get it back; the
  working-memory rule says don't make them hold it. *Fix:* a small "‹ ›" line
  counter for multi-beat moments and a "repeat" affordance. *Command:*
  `/impeccable clarify`.
- **[P2] Pointing target relies on text.**
  *Why it matters:* the marker helps, but on mobile the marker can sit under the
  thumb or off-screen. *Fix:* if the anchor is off-screen, nudge the camera to
  frame the building before pointing (the campus `focus()` API already exists,
  `world.ts:300`). *Command:* `/impeccable adapt`.
- **[P2] Mobile advisor crowds the bottom-left field.**
  *Why it matters:* Casey (below) is one-handed; the advisor and the date bar
  share the thumb zone. *Fix:* after 2 advances, collapse to the portrait only
  and let a tap re-expand. *Command:* `/impeccable adapt`.
